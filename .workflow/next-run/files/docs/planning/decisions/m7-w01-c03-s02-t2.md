# M7-W01-C03-S02-T2 — finite PlotModel boundary and renderer ownership disposition

## Question

Do RS113's finite reciprocal point/segment oracles establish enough evidence to publish a Math PlotModel API, adopt an adaptive sampling engine or integrate chart rendering? What finite domain, byte/point/segment, cancellation, typed partial-result and ownership evidence is required for a future operation-level admission?

## Observation

- RS113's source-pinned `docs/planning/evidence/M7-W01-C03-S02-T1-qualified.json` independently accepted one declared `1/x` grid `[-2,-1,0,1,2]` with **four finite points**, **two disconnected segments**, **one recognized pole gap**, and **one break**. The grid `[-2,-1,1,2]`, with zero omitted, also retains exactly one break: no polyline is permitted to bridge the *known* pole.
- The local BCL-only `docs/planning/ValidateM7PlotSampling.cs` refuses too few/many samples, duplicate/reversed or nonfinite positions, and nonfinite reciprocal output. Accepted *research limits* are **8 samples / 8 points / 2 segments**. Any rejected call exposes **zero partial points, segments or gaps**.
- A pair of binary64 coordinates theoretically contains 16 scalar bytes; 8 such point pairs account for **128 bytes of coordinate payload**, **not** managed-array overhead, metadata, temporary buffers or an audited memory allocation bound. No general byte cap, heap/CPU budget, deadline/cancellation guarantee, numerical error certificate, or partial-output policy has been qualified for a public plotting implementation.
- S01's owner-gated pure DAG spike finished by an explicit runtime *deferral* in `docs/planning/evidence/M7-W01-C03-S01-T2-qualified.json`. It does not provide a PlotModel executor. The original parent size-L C03 remains planned until its own aggregate tasks and independent acceptance are reconciled.
- A real consuming Math operation, exact immutable PlotModel public contract, product tests, source-free NuGet consumer, new provider identity/rights, or AURA/chart rendering authorization has not been demonstrated. Two finite BCL witnesses are evidence of a narrow domain, not proof of general discontinuity detection.

## Decision — defer PlotModel and renderer admission

**DEFER a public PlotModel/Plot API, adaptive sampler, generic discontinuity/continuity inference, graphical rendering and third-party graphics/sampling backends.** **No public PlotModel** or graphics renderer has been delivered. Preserve the locally qualified research oracle only, with the known `1/x` pole and conservative no-partial-result refusals. This closes the S02 **research spike** without inventing a product capability, a new package or a permanent prohibition.

Three mandatory **review triggers** must be independently met before any future owner-authorized admission:

1. **Documented consumer gap:** name an actual Math caller and exact scalar function, domain, known discontinuities, sample-grid/segment output contract and why existing Math APIs cannot satisfy the need. No inferred samples, interpolation or arbitrary-function continuity claim.
2. **Finite semantics, budgets and refusals:** state immutable point/segment identity, double precision policy, sorted finite inputs, known-pole break topology, coordinate and total allocation bytes, point/segment/time caps, cancellation and deadline behavior, typed invalid/nonfinite failures and explicit accept/reject policy for partial results. No unqualified bridging or hidden drawing.
3. **Provider/recipient and ownership qualification:** pin exact source/version/commit SHA, license, notices, transitive/native rights and .NET/RID if an external engine is proposed; prove offline restore/rollback, independently checked adversarial discontinuity oracles, an isolated **source-free consumer**, and explicit agreement from the Math and actual graphics/client owners.

**All three review triggers are necessary**, but their existence never substitutes for a new DWF admission decision. If any is missing, **remain deferred** with these exact reassessment conditions.

## Owner and integration boundaries

Math may own admitted numeric point/segment data. Actual chart drawing, axes, UI widgets, themes, interaction, job/task execution, resource governance and permission controls remain with **AURA or the consuming client**. No AURA job scheduler, render engine, PlotModel runtime, graphics package, Decision/MCDM API, `dw.discovery` exposure or public NuGet was installed or updated.

The reference limit `8 samples / 8 points / 2 segments` and illustrative 128 bytes do not constitute a shipped performance guarantee. One `1/x` example does not license universal continuity detection, statistical measurement uncertainty or certified numerical error.
