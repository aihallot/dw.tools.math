# M7-W02-C01-T1 — finite polynomial identity certificate and formal interoperability screen

## Question

What exactly can Math attest when comparing Lean, Rocq and SMT workflows for a small algebraic identity without installing a theorem prover, assuming a solver is trusted, or mistaking sampled equality for a checked proof? What status must a failure, proof hole or timeout retain?

## Observation — independently replayed finite certificate

The authoritative research fixture is `docs/planning/fixtures/m7-polynomial-identity-certificate.json`. It declares the identity

`(x - 1)(x + 1) = x^2 - 1`

over the **exact integer polynomial ring `Z[x]`**, with **ascending-degree coefficients**. The two left factors are `[-1, 1]` and `[1, 1]`; the claimed right-hand and certificate product vectors are both `[-1, 0, 1]`. An independently authored, source-only C# BCL program, `docs/planning/ValidateM7FormalCertificate.cs`, replays integer polynomial multiplication using `BigInteger` convolution and accepts this exact coefficient equality.

This proves only the finite declared algebraic identity **relative to the explicitly documented trusted base** (local verifier logic and BCL integer arithmetic). It does **not** establish an independent Lean kernel proof, a Rocq proof term, an SMT certificate, a general-purpose symbolic prover, a mathematically certified .NET runtime or a qualified source-free API consumer.

The checker must reject a deliberately altered `x` coefficient or missing term as an invalid certificate, and classify **proof hole**, **timeout** and **external-provider failure** as **unproved**, never as success, even if finite numerical examples agree. The C# verification is read-only, bounded to the fixed small fixture and does not download or invoke any external proof assistant.

## Comparison and distinction

| Candidate | Potential interoperable evidence | Missing qualification |
|---|---|---|
| **Lean** | Kernel-checkable proof term in a pinned Lean toolchain | Actual toolchain/version/hash/license, trusted kernel, offline replay, target/runtime and source-free consumer |
| **Rocq** | Kernel-checkable proof term in a pinned Rocq toolchain | Actual installed version/hash/license, trusted kernel and independent replay/certificate export |
| **SMT workflow** | An explicit proof/certificate format replayed by a separate sound checker | Named solver version/license, certificate specification, checker trusted base; SAT/UNKNOWN/TIMEOUT alone never certify a theorem |
| **Local BCL finite certificate** | Replayed identity coefficient vector `[-1,0,1]`, exact convolution; negative statuses | **Research-only witness**, not a general prover or candidate provider acceptance |

The *derivation* explains algebraic reasoning; the *certificate* is the finite machine-readable coefficient witness; *verification* independently checks its content and source. A solver's claim, passed point samples, a numerical residual or a process exit status alone does **not** constitute an independently verified proof.

## Decision — admit research witness only

**QUALIFY** this one finite `Z[x]` certificate and explicit negative verdict semantics for T1. **DEFER** external proof toolchain selection, general proof-term import/export, theorem-prover integration, new formal APIs and package publishing until a specific consumer, licensing/trusted base, platform/TFM/RID and isolated offline certificate replay are qualified.

No new `dw.tools.math.formal` product project, native/third-party runtime, AURA execution dependency, public NuGet package or `dw.discovery` callable proof interface is introduced. Historical M7-W01 research decisions and their provider deferrals remain intact.

## T2 gates and exact limits

`M7-W02-C01-T2` must independently review the declared certificate kind and bounded trusted base, hole/timeout/error propagation, proof versus certificate versus derivation terminology, actual provider/version/source/license/RID/rights if proposed, complexity and total CPU/memory/time policies, a source-free recipient and explicit ownership approval. **Unproved stays unproved**, and no home-grown theorem prover may be built to satisfy an integration label.

On successful RS118, `M7-W02` and `M7-W02-C01` become **in-progress**, `M7-W02-C01-T1` and its three Q/O/D subtasks become **done**, C01-T2 and C02 remain **not-ready**, and M7 stays in progress. No solver or kernel is claimed certified.

**Cost stop-loss:** one machine-readable fixture, one read-only C# verifier, two English research documents, targeted native planning checks. No provider downloads, full suite, CI, sibling changes or broad proof benchmark.
