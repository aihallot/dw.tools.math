## Decision — close the parent research spike with explicit deferral

**QUALIFY original M7-W01-C03 as completed research only, with both S01 DAG runtime and S02 PlotModel/renderer product admissions DEFERRED.** This is a reproducible disposition of the accepted size-L work, not a new engine, proof of arbitrary expression safety, universal discontinuity detector, or permanent prohibition.

Three **mandatory future review triggers** must be independently satisfied before any owner-authorized implementation:

1. **Named consumer and operation:** define a real Math or client caller, immutable pure DAG and/or point/segment operations, finite scalar/domain and known-singularity assumptions, and demonstrate a measured gap in currently admitted Math contracts.
2. **Total bounded semantics:** define graph aliases and purity, deterministic dependency order and cycle refusal, no-partial-result and cancellation/deadline contract; separately define monotone finite sample positions, explicit pole breaks, point/segment bounds, **total allocation byte** cap (not coordinate payload), checked computation, numeric error/domain, ownership and typed refusal statuses. No automatic general interpolation, implicit remote scheduler or hidden partial values.
3. **Provider, source-free recipient and ownership approval:** identify exact code/package source SHA/version/license/transitive notices, actual .NET target/RID, offline restore and rollback, a standalone source-free nupkg consumer and adversarial oracles; obtain explicit Math and AURA/client owner approval for any interface or renderer.

**All three review triggers are necessary, never automatic approval.** When any is missing, the public DAG/PlotModel/rendering requests **remain deferred**; a future distinct DWF decision may authorize only its qualified subset.

## Math versus AURA and downstream nonclaims

Math owns admitted pure computation, exact/numeric meaning and immutable mathematical *data*. **AURA or consuming clients own job/agent scheduling, resource orchestration, UI charts, rendering, permissions, transport and presentation.** No sibling repository is modified or assumed to adopt a feature.

There is **no public DAG API**, **no public PlotModel API**, no adaptive plotting, GPU/native provider, copied AURA job engine, renderer, qualified new source/rights/RID, new product test suite, advanced package, `dw.discovery` exposure or published NuGet release. The research-only sample bounds cannot be cited as product service levels, certified floating-point error or physical measurement uncertainty.
